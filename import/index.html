Reference · Customs B Maritime Import

Custom Total Value (CIP)

The customs value declared for a CIP import is the sum of the goods' value, transport costs, FOB surcharges and insurance.

Value of Goods
Transports Cost
Surcharges Cost (FOB)
Insurance
Example: Operation from Barcelona Port to Rotterdam (Maritime)
Value of the goods: 55,000 € (Invoice)
Inland Transport: from customer warehouse to Port Origin (BCN), 25 km × 18 € = 450 €
Main Transport: Maritime vessel freight from BCN to RTM = 600 € (check SDG Lines)
Surcharges to complete FOB = THC (150) + DOC (45) + Port Taxes (25) + VGM (35) = 255 €
Insurance = Flat fee — 500 €
Custom Total Value = 55,000 + 450 + 600 + 255 + 500 = 56,805 €
Instructions: once you have the total value, send an email to international@escolaeuropea.eu with the subject line "Customs Value_'TEAMname'", indicating each of the fields used to obtain the value. Attach the commercial invoice and the quotation from the shipping company.

Price Indicator

  • Price per km = 18 € > 450 (1.8 per km)
  • Insurance = 500 €
  • Freight = SDG Lines
  • Surcharges = SDG Lines
