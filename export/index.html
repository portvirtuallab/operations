Cross-cutting element Incoterms 2020

Incoterm CIP — Carriage and Insurance Paid To

Physical route and cost / risk allocation applicable to the entire export process, regardless of the mode of transport.

Seller's cost
Risk transfer
Buyer's cost
Insurance (+ Insurance) — paid by the seller
+ Insurance (covers the entire journey)
Sea Freight Air Freight Rail Road Multimodal — Any combination

CIP applies to any mode of transport or combination thereof. The seller's cost (orange) covers up to loading on board (On Board), the point at which the risk transfer (grey) to the buyer also takes place. The buyer's cost (green) begins on arrival and extends to their warehouse. The insurance (red, + Insurance) covers the whole marked route and is paid by the seller, regardless of where the risk is transferred.
