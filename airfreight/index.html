Incoterms 2020 — Carriage Paid To (CPT)

Seller's cost
Transfer of risk
Buyer's cost
Transportation Modes: ALL

CPT applies to any mode of transport or combination thereof. Unlike CIP, it does not include insurance — the seller's cost (orange) covers transport up to the agreed destination, while risk (grey) transfers to the buyer once the goods are handed to the first carrier. The buyer's cost (green) begins on arrival at destination.

Step 1 · Point B Airfreight Export

Custom Total Value (CIP)

Whenever the operation requires a CIP-based customs value, the quotation must combine the value of the goods with all transport, surcharge, and insurance costs.

B
Value of Goods
Transports Cost
Surcharges Cost (FOB)
Insurance
Example: Operation from Barcelona Port to Rotterdam (Maritime)
Value of the goods: 55,000 € (Invoice)
Inland Transport: from customer warehouse to Port Origin (BCN), 25 km × 18 € = 450 €
Main Transport: Maritime vessel freight from BCN to RTM = 600 € (check SDG Lines)
Surcharges to complete FOB = THC (150) + DOC (45) + Port Taxes (25) + VGM (35) = 255 €
Insurance = Plain Fee — 500 €
Custom Total Value = 55,000 + 450 + 600 + 255 + 500 = 56,805 €

Price indicator

  • Price per km = 18 € > 450 (1.8 per km)
  • Insurance = 500 €
  • Freight = SDG Lines
  • Surcharges = SDG Lines
Instructions: Once you have the total value, send an email to international@escolaeuropea.eu with the subject line "Customs Value_'TEAMname'", indicating each of the fields you used to obtain the value. Please attach the commercial invoice and the quotation from the shipping company.
